Before disposal

Before you recycle something that still works

1
Sell it
Useful laptops, phones, consoles and cameras may still have value.
2
Donate or give it away
A working low-value item may be more useful to another person than as scrap.
3
Repair it
Hudson Valley Repair Café programs may be worth checking for repairable gear.
Large quantities

Have a carload of electronics?

A few old devices and a large load are not always handled the same way. For a carload or larger quantity, contact the destination before arriving rather than assuming ordinary drop-off rules apply.

About this directory

We organize publicly available information from recycling agencies, municipalities, retailers and take-back programs. We are not UCRRA, Ulster County government, a municipal transfer station or an electronics recycler.

Hours, fees, accepted items and eligibility can change. Verify important details with the destination before traveling. Directory pages should cite the primary municipal, UCRRA, retailer or NY DEC source used for each rule. Independent directory · verified against cited public sources.
