What this directory is, what it is not, and how to use the information safely.

Independent directory

Ulster E-Waste Directory is an independent informational website. It is not affiliated with Ulster County, UCRRA, NYSDEC, any municipality, retailer, manufacturer, recycler, transfer station, or other listed organization unless explicitly stated.

Information can change

Recycling and disposal rules are operational information. Facilities may change hours, fees, permits, accepted items, seasonal schedules, residency rules, quantity limits, or closures without notice. The directory cites official sources where practical, but visitors should verify before traveling.

Classification matters

Not every object containing electronics is handled as ordinary e-waste. Batteries, appliances, vehicle components, refrigerant-containing equipment, hazardous materials, and other items may follow different rules. Follow the destination's current instructions.

Data-bearing devices

For computers, phones, tablets, storage drives, game consoles, and similar devices, you are responsible for handling personal data before resale, donation, repair, recycling, or disposal.
