Privacy Policy

A short explanation of what this directory does—and does not—collect.

Information you provide

This site does not currently offer user accounts, lead forms, uploads, or direct-contact forms. We do not ask visitors to submit names, phone numbers, addresses, or other personal information through the directory.

Automatic technical data

Like most websites, hosting, security, analytics, or advertising services may automatically receive limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring page, pages viewed, and approximate location derived from network information.

This site may display advertising. Advertising providers and other third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies to measure ad delivery, prevent fraud, limit repeated ads, or understand site usage. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

External links

The directory links to municipalities, UCRRA, New York State agencies, retailers, manufacturers, recyclers, and other external websites. We do not control their privacy practices.

Children

This directory is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the site adds analytics, advertising, forms, or other features. The current version applies to the site as it exists today.
