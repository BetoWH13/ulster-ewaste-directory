Winter Electronics Recycling

UCRRA’s regular Kingston electronics collection is seasonal. When that program is closed, use year-round municipal, retailer, manufacturer or other verified alternatives instead of assuming there is nowhere to go.

Choose a winter route in this order

  1. Check a municipal route you are eligible to use. Residency and permits can matter more than distance.
  2. Check the manufacturer’s New York take-back program. Manufacturers must provide a free and reasonably convenient route for covered equipment under the state program.
  3. Check a registered collection site or retailer. Call ahead because a listed site may limit brands, device types, quantities, or services.
  4. Store the device safely if no verified route is open. Covered electronics do not belong in household trash or at curbside for trash pickup.

Retailer and manufacturer routes

New York manufacturer programs and some retailer take-back options can operate outside the seasonal county collection window. Acceptance varies by device and program, so check the specific item before traveling.

If you need to wait for spring

  • Keep devices indoors or securely covered, dry, and protected from breakage.
  • Do not dismantle a television, monitor, computer, or other device to make it easier to store.
  • Separate loose rechargeable batteries and use a verified battery take-back route.
  • Keep damaged or swollen lithium-ion batteries away from heat and ordinary trash, and ask the receiving program how to handle them.
  • Remove personal data before surrendering computers, phones, tablets, drives, or consoles.

Why UCRRA is different

UCRRA’s Kingston electronics collection is the strong county route during its operating season. Outside that season, the directory should route you elsewhere rather than pretending the program is year-round.

Winter trip checklist

  • Confirm the date and weather closure status.
  • Confirm residency or permit eligibility.
  • Name the exact device when you call.
  • Ask about quantity and size limits.
  • Keep a backup destination in mind.
