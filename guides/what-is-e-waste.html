What Is — and Isn’t — E‑Waste?

Electronics-containing does not automatically mean e-waste. Batteries, microwaves, small appliances and vehicle components can follow completely different systems.

Oddballs still matter

UCRRA accepts items such as cameras and GPS/navigation devices even though their statutory treatment can differ from the most familiar covered equipment categories. The directory follows the verified local acceptance rule, not a simplistic “has electronics = e-waste” test.

Pepe test

If Pepe holds up a specific thing, the directory should tell him which system it belongs to before telling him where to drive.
