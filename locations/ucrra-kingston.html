Before you drive over

  • Use the designated electronics collection point and check in every time.
  • Do not leave electronics after hours.
  • No electronics collection is held on holidays.
  • For a carload or larger quantity, contact UCRRA before arriving.
  • If the device still works, selling, donating or repairing it may be better than recycling it immediately.

Season matters

This is a seasonal Saturday program. If you need to recycle electronics outside April–November, use the directory’s town, retailer and manufacturer alternatives.

See winter options

Large quantity?

A large load is not something to treat like an ordinary one-item drop-off. Contact UCRRA before arriving with a carload or larger quantity.
