CRT and LCD monitors

Cell phones
Servers, keyboards and mice
VCRs, DVD players and digital music players
Cable/satellite receivers and converter boxes
Stereo components and radios

The rules also describe additional electronics and oversized equipment. Contact the facility before bringing dismantled, scientific, medical, business, or unusually heavy equipment.

Before you drive over

  • Bring the required access permit and an accepted non-cash payment method if purchasing anything.
  • Keep screens and devices intact unless the facility confirms otherwise.
  • Ask first about oversized items, mixed loads, or loose batteries.
  • Handle personal data before dropping off computers, phones, drives, or consoles.

Broad local acceptance

Shawangunk publishes one of the county’s broader municipal electronics lists, including TVs, computers, phones, game consoles, and common peripherals.
