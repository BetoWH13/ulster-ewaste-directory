Electronics acceptance

The Town’s latest posted transfer-station materials include electronic waste among accepted categories, and the July 2025 update specifically confirms televisions and monitors.

Electronic waste
Televisions
Computer monitors

For less-common electronics, check directly with the transfer station before traveling; the newer public update is strongest for TVs and monitors.

Permit and payment rules

  • Permit required.
  • Latest posted brochure lists resident and non-resident permits.
  • One-time drop-off option is listed.
  • Cash is not accepted.
  • Latest brochure lists check, credit card, debit card, punch card and money order as payment methods.

Closure note

The Town says that when a holiday falls on Monday, the transfer station is closed the following day. For inclement weather, check the Town website or Town of Hurley social updates for closures or delays.

Useful for Woodstock-area users

Hurley is a credible nearby permit-based alternative for Woodstock-area residents when UCRRA’s seasonal program is not the best fit.
