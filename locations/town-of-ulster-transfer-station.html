Electronics listed by the Town

The electronic-waste notice linked from the Town’s current transfer-station page lists these categories:

Computers and small servers
Televisions
Printers, scanners and fax machines
Video game consoles
Keyboards, mice and speakers
VCRs, DVRs and DVD players
Telephones and digital music players
Cable boxes, satellite receivers and converter boxes
UPS battery backups
Ink cartridges and small batteries

Battery categories can follow different rules. Verify loose-battery acceptance directly before relying on the broad wording in the Town notice.

Before you drive over

  • Confirm that your permit is current.
  • Call if the device is dismantled, unusually large, leaking, or not clearly listed.
  • Handle personal data before surrendering computers, phones, drives, or consoles.
  • Use UCRRA Kingston as a county alternative when its seasonal program is operating.

Good fit for

Town of Ulster residents and other eligible permit holders with ordinary covered electronics such as TVs, computers, printers, and consoles.
