Reuse before disposal

The ReUse Center accepts many clean, unbroken household goods and surplus materials when space and condition allow. Electronics are not treated as ordinary ReUse donations: the published guidance routes listed electronics to free e-waste recycling instead.

Why this matters: a working lamp, houseware or building material may belong in reuse; a laptop or TV belongs in the e-waste stream.

Load limits

  • The facility describes itself as non-commercial.
  • Recycling loads are limited to one yard per week.
  • Garbage loads are limited to no more than two yards.
  • Large loads should go to UCRRA in New Paltz or Kingston.

Microwaves and small appliances are different

The Town’s current recycling guidance separates mostly-metal small appliances such as microwaves and toaster ovens into the scrap metal stream rather than ordinary e-waste. The fee schedule also lists appliance charges separately.
