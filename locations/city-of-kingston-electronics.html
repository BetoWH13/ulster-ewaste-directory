City of Kingston Electronics Recycling

Kingston residents can bring electronic equipment to the City transfer station for free. The city also offers paid curbside electronics collection.

Small electronics
Televisions
CRT TVs / monitors
Flat-screen TVs
Consoles
Large printers
Routers
Other accepted e-waste

The city says transfer-station electronics drop-off is free for residents; curbside electronics are currently listed at $20 per item.

Before you drive over

  • Bring electronics to the transfer station if you want the free resident drop-off route.
  • Saturday operation is contingent on staff availability according to the 2026 calendar.
  • Curbside electronics pickup requires advance payment.
  • Do not confuse small appliances with e-waste: the City FAQ treats microwaves and many small appliances differently.

Great winter backup

This is one of the important year-round alternatives when UCRRA’s seasonal electronics program is closed.
