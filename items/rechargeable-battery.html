Rechargeable Battery

Rechargeable batteries follow a separate battery take-back system. Do not treat a loose battery like ordinary e-waste, and do not put it in household trash.

Best first move

Use a rechargeable-battery take-back location or other approved battery collection route. Qualifying retailers that sell covered rechargeable batteries have take-back obligations under New York law.

Before you transport it

  • Identify the battery type if possible.
  • Check whether the destination accepts that chemistry.
  • Keep terminals protected when required.
  • If swollen, damaged or leaking, check specific handling rules before traveling.
  • For large quantities, contact the destination first.

Separate New York law

Rechargeable batteries are covered by a separate New York rechargeable-battery recycling law, distinct from the main consumer e-waste program.
