Best first move

Working chargers and useful cables are often more valuable as a bundle than as individual resale items. Give away what still works; recycle dead or obsolete accessories through a verified route.

Sell / donateUsually better to bundle or give away than sell individually.
RepairUsually not worth repairing ordinary low-value accessories.
DataUsually none.
ClassificationAssociated wires and cables are accepted by some electronics programs.

Do not do this

Do not assume every loose accessory is accepted just because a facility accepts computers.

Before recycling

  • Can you bundle useful cables with the device they belong to?
  • Would a neighbor, school or reuse group take working accessories?
  • Is the charger damaged or unsafe?
  • Are you bringing a handful or a large box?
