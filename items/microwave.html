Microwave Oven

A microwave contains electronics, but that does not make it ordinary e-waste. In Ulster County it generally follows an appliance or scrap-metal route.

Best first move

If it still works, donate or give it away. If it is dead, use an appliance or scrap-metal route rather than assuming an electronics program wants it.

Sell / donateWorking units may be useful to someone else.
RepairUsually depends on age, value and fault.
DataNone.
ClassificationExcluded from New York’s covered consumer electronics list; handle as appliance/scrap/disposal.

Do not do this

Do not take a microwave to an ordinary e-waste event simply because it has electronic controls.

Before disposal

  • Does it still work?
  • Would someone take it for reuse?
  • Is it mostly metal?
  • Does the local facility treat it as scrap or appliance waste?
  • Are there any fees?
