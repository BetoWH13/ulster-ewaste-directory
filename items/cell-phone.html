Best first move

Phones are often worth selling or trading in. If you recycle one, remove your data and use a phone-specific take-back or electronics route.

Sell / donateExcellent resale or trade-in candidate when functional.
RepairOften worthwhile for screens, batteries and other common faults.
DataVery high. Sign out, back up and wipe the device before transfer.
ClassificationPhones are handled differently from the main NY covered-electronics list and have a separate wireless-phone recycling framework.

Do not do this

Do not recycle or sell a phone without removing accounts and personal data.

Before you recycle it

  • Does it still work?
  • Would someone realistically buy or use it?
  • Is repair sensible?
  • Does it contain personal data?
  • Are you bringing one item or a large quantity?

This directory keeps those checks brief. The main job is still to route you to the right local place or program.
