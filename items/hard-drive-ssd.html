Best first move

For storage devices, the data question comes first. Wipe or securely destroy sensitive data before recycling or handing the drive to anyone else.

Sell / reuseUsable drives can sometimes be reused after secure erasure.
RepairUsually not economical for ordinary consumer drives.
DataCritical. Assume personal files remain until securely erased or destroyed.
ClassificationExternal drives are explicitly covered electronics; removed internal drives can require more specific handling.

Do not do this

Do not toss a drive into a recycling bin while assuming deletion or formatting automatically made the data unrecoverable.

Secure destruction

For especially sensitive data, a paid secure-destruction service may be worth more than a free recycling route because the service is about documented data destruction, not merely material recovery.

Before you recycle it

  • Is the drive still usable?
  • Have you backed up anything important?
  • Have you securely erased or destroyed sensitive data?
  • Is it an external drive or a removed internal drive?
  • Are you bringing one drive or a large quantity?
