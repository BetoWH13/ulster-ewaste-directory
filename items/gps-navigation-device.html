GPS / Navigation Device

Older GPS units may have limited resale value, but working devices can still be reused or donated. UCRRA explicitly accepts GPS/navigation devices.

Best first move

Working GPS/navigation devices can sometimes be sold or given away. Recycling should be the end-of-life route.

Sell / donatePossible for newer or specialized navigation devices.
RepairUsually limited unless the unit has meaningful value.
DataClear saved addresses, routes and personal locations before transfer.

Do not do this

Do not hand off a GPS unit while home addresses, saved destinations or personal routes remain on it.
