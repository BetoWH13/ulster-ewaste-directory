Best first move

If it still works, giving it away may be easier than selling it. Dead printers and scanners can use qualifying electronics recycling routes.

Sell / donateUsually low resale value; donation or give-away may be more practical.
RepairOnly worthwhile when the device has real value or a simple fault.
DataUsually low, but some office devices store scan/fax/address-book data.
ClassificationMany qualifying printers and scanners are covered electronics.

Do not do this

Large office machines can fall outside ordinary consumer assumptions; check weight and program rules.

Before you recycle it

  • Does it still work?
  • Would someone realistically buy or use it?
  • Is repair sensible?
  • Does it contain personal data?
  • Are you bringing one item or a large quantity?

This directory keeps those checks brief. The main job is still to route you to the right local place or program.
