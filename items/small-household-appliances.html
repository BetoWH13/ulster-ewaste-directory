Small Household Appliances

Vacuum cleaners, coffee makers, blenders, toasters and similar appliances are not automatically e-waste. The right route may be repair, reuse, scrap metal or ordinary disposal.

Best first move

If it still works, donate or give it away. If it is broken, decide whether repair is practical; otherwise use the local scrap-metal, appliance or disposal route that matches the item.

Sell / donateWorking items often have more reuse value than scrap value.
RepairWorth considering for vacuums and better-quality appliances.
DataUsually none.
ClassificationNot automatically covered e-waste just because the item has a cord or circuit board.

Do not do this

Do not send every plugged-in household device to an electronics recycler. Appliance and scrap rules can be different.

Think in this order

  • Does it still work?
  • Can it be repaired?
  • Can someone reuse it?
  • Is it mostly metal?
  • Does it contain refrigerant or another special component?
  • What does the local facility call this item?
