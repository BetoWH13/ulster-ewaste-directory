Best first move

Working consoles are often worth selling before recycling. Remove account information and stored data first.

Sell / donateStrong resale potential for recent or collectible systems.
RepairCan be worthwhile for common hardware faults.
DataModerate to high. Accounts, payment methods and saves may remain.
ClassificationUse a qualifying electronics recycling route when end-of-life.

Do not do this

Do not hand off a console while your account, saved passwords or payment information remain on it.
