Best first move

If the TV still works, sell or give it away first. If it is dead, use a TV/electronics recycling route and check acceptance before driving.

Sell / donateWorking TVs may still be worth selling or giving away.
RepairUsually a case-by-case decision depending on age and fault.
DataUsually low, though smart TVs can hold accounts or settings.
ClassificationCovered electronic equipment.

Do not do this

Do not assume every retailer or town transfer station accepts TVs.

Before you recycle it

  • Does it still work?
  • Would someone realistically buy or use it?
  • Is repair sensible?
  • Does it contain personal data?
  • Are you bringing one item or a large quantity?

This directory keeps those checks brief. The main job is still to route you to the right local place or program.

Season matters

UCRRA's regular Kingston electronics collection is seasonal, so winter alternatives may matter depending on the item and town.
