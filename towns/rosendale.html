Rosendale Transfer Station context

The local facility operates a recycling center and trash collection site, but its electronics section sends listed devices to UCRRA.

  • Address: 414 Whiteport Road, Kingston, NY 12401
  • Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday: 7:30 AM–3:10 PM
  • Wednesday: 11:30 AM–3:10 PM
  • Permit: required for local facility use
  • 2026 full residential permit: $50
  • 2026 limited residential permit: $30

Devices the Town routes to UCRRA

Examples include monitors, terminals, typewriters, printers, scanners, cables, televisions, copiers, modems, telephones, VCRs, DVD players, CRTs, stereos, radios, CPUs, keyboards, fax machines, tape recorders, compact-disc players, answering machines, and video game players.

Rosendale answer

Use the Town’s UCRRA routing for ordinary electronics, then confirm the UCRRA season, hours, and accepted-item list before traveling.

Winter route

When UCRRA’s regular program is closed, use a verified retailer, manufacturer, or eligible year-round municipal option for the specific device.
