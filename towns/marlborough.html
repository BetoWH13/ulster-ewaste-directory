Electronics Recycling in Marlborough, NY

The Town of Marlborough Transfer Station explicitly says it does not accept electronics. Residents need a county, retailer, or manufacturer alternative.

Best current routes

Marlborough Transfer Station — not for electronicsThe Town’s current page says the facility does not accept electronics. Do not buy a permit or load a vehicle assuming TVs, computers, or printers can go there.
UCRRA Electronics Recycling — KingstonFree seasonal county program for listed electronics, Saturdays 8 AM–2 PM, April–November.
See UCRRA Kingston
Retailer or manufacturer take-backUseful when UCRRA is out of season or when a device has a product-specific take-back route. Confirm the exact item and quantity before traveling.
Appliances are different: Marlborough lists microwaves and other appliances under its fee-based appliance rules even though it rejects electronics. Follow the facility’s category, not a simple “has a plug” test.

Marlborough answer

Do not take electronics to the Town transfer station. Use UCRRA Kingston during its season or a verified item-specific take-back route.
