Why the distinction matters

New York prohibits covered electronics from ordinary trash and requires qualifying manufacturers to provide take-back programs. Those rules do not make every municipal transfer station accept every device. This page reports local acceptance only when an official local source says so.

Denning answer

Do not bring electronics to Denning’s transfer station. Use UCRRA in season or a verified manufacturer/collection-site route.

Before traveling

  • Confirm the exact device, especially televisions, CRTs, batteries, appliances, or damaged equipment.
  • Ask about residency, permits, fees, quantity limits, and the last vehicle check-in.
  • Use the manufacturer or a registered collection site when a local program cannot take the item.
