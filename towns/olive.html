Olive Transfer Station details

  • Address: 580 Beaverkill Road, Olivebridge, NY 12461
  • Hours: Tuesday–Saturday · 7:30 AM–3:00 PM
  • Closed: Sunday and Monday
  • Access: Olive residents only
  • Electronics: not accepted

Olive answer

Do not bring electronics to the Olive Transfer Station. Use UCRRA Kingston in season or confirm another eligible route.

Classification check

Metal appliances, refrigerant appliances, and ordinary electronics follow different rules. Start with the specific item rather than treating everything with a cord as e-waste.
