Electronics Recycling in Woodstock, NY

Woodstock does not appear to operate its own verified electronics drop-off. The practical answer is to use nearby county, municipal, or permit-based alternatives — and avoid stale guidance that still points to Saugerties for e-waste.

Best current routes from Woodstock

1. UCRRA Electronics Recycling — KingstonFree seasonal electronics recycling at 999 Flatbush Road, Saturdays 8 AM–2 PM, April–November. Large quantities should contact UCRRA first.
See UCRRA Kingston
2. Hurley Transfer Station & Recycling Center — West HurleyA nearby permit-based alternative at 1043 Dug Hill Road. A July 2025 Town update says TVs and monitors are again accepted free of charge. For less-common electronics, verify acceptance before traveling.
See Hurley details
3. Saugerties Transfer Station — do not bring electronics thereWoodstock residents may use the Saugerties transfer station under its permit system, but Saugerties currently says it has not accepted electronics since January 1, 2023.
See Saugerties alternatives
Why this page matters: Woodstock’s own Green Guide still says the Saugerties Transfer Station has electronics recycling. That guidance is stale. Saugerties’ current transfer-station page says electronics are not accepted there.

If the item still works

  • Sell higher-value items such as laptops, phones, consoles and cameras first.
  • Give away or donate low-value working electronics when practical.
  • Repair can make sense before disposal, especially for small appliances and some computers.
  • Then use the directory when the item is truly end-of-life.

Woodstock answer in one line

No verified town e-waste drop-off of its own. Use UCRRA Kingston or another verified nearby program; do not rely on the old Saugerties electronics reference.

Season problem?

UCRRA Kingston is seasonal. Outside April–November, check item-specific retailer/manufacturer options or a year-round permit-based facility such as Hurley if eligible.
