Saugerties electronics recycling options

The important answer here is a clean one: do not bring ordinary electronics to the Saugerties Transfer Station. The Town says electronics have not been accepted there since January 1, 2023.

The current rule

No electronics accepted. The Town of Saugerties says its Transfer Station has not accepted electronics since January 1, 2023.

That makes this page useful even though the local answer is “no.” The goal is to keep residents from making a wasted trip and route them to the right alternatives.

The transfer station remains useful for non-e-waste categories, but not for ordinary electronics.

Where should electronics go instead?

  • UCRRA Kingston: seasonal county electronics program for TVs, computers, monitors and many other devices.
  • Staples Kingston: the Town points residents there for many electronics such as computers and printers; exclusions apply.
  • Manufacturer / retailer take-back: useful for certain covered electronics and for winter options.
See UCRRA Kingston

Useful boundary rules

The fact that Saugerties says “no electronics” does not mean it says “no” to every object with a cord or battery.

Weather note

The Town warns that severe winter storms can close the transfer station. Call or check the Town before traveling during bad weather.
